Hello! I am Sungjun Lim, a graduate researcher at Yonsei University's Statistics and Data Science department.

My research focuses on Bayesian Deep Learning and Large Language Models, with a particular interest in developing robust and interpretable AI systems.

I am currently working on uncertainty quantification in deep learning models and their applications in real-world scenarios.
